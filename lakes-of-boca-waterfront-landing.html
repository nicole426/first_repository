About This Home

This exceptional waterfront pool home showcases the very best of Lakes of Boca Raton living. Set on a prestigious 13,000 square foot cul-de-sac lot, this residence offers an unparalleled combination of privacy, luxury, and stunning natural beauty.

The home features 4 spacious bedrooms plus a versatile den/office and loft area, along with 3.5 bathrooms thoughtfully designed for modern family living. The first-floor primary suite provides convenient single-level living, while the additional bedrooms and loft offer ample space for family and guests.

Impact windows throughout provide peace of mind and energy efficiency, while newer A/C systems ensure year-round comfort. The screened covered patio is perfect for entertaining while enjoying the breathtaking three-way lake views, and the spacious backyard offers endless possibilities for outdoor enjoyment.

Lakes of Boca Raton is a premier community offering resort-style amenities including a clubhouse, pool, fitness center, and courts for tennis, pickleball, and basketball. All of this is situated within a highly sought-after A-rated school district, making it the perfect place to call home.
